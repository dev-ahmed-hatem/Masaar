01

Vision & scope

Students browse approved teachers, request a lesson slot, the teacher confirms, and the live lesson happens on an external tool. Teachers are individual profiles — no academies or multi-tenant brands.

There is no recorded course library, no LMS, and no content DRM at launch. The only teacher media is a short intro video hosted on YouTube and played via the Vidstack player on the web.

Non-goals at launch Out

  • Payment gateways — manual only for the first launch.
  • Recorded courses, quizzes, assignments, gradebook.
  • Academies, co-teachers, teaching assistants.
  • VAT / formal tax e-invoicing.
  • In-platform video calling (we attach external links only).
02

Markets & localization

Egypt (EGP) and Saudi Arabia (SAR) run as parallel, mostly-siloed markets on one platform. Market is a first-class entity: teachers, students, prices, payment accounts, and payouts are all scoped to a market.

  • Per-market teachers & currency — Egyptian teachers serve Egyptian students (paid EGP); Saudi teachers serve Saudi students (paid SAR). No cross-market booking at launch.
  • Locales — Arabic (RTL) + English (LTR), full RTL layout across all surfaces, not just translated strings.
  • Time zones — store UTC, display local. Egypt UTC+2 (DST → +3 in summer); Saudi UTC+3 (no DST). Slot logic must be timezone-correct.
  • Money — stored as integer minor units + ISO currency code; never mix currencies in one ledger.

Authentication

Phone number + OTP, delivered via WhatsApp only (no SMS at launch). Applies to students primarily; teacher/moderator web login also uses phone + WhatsApp OTP. Session/device management and OTP rate-limiting required.

05

Catalog & taxonomy

Three verticals, each with its own level taxonomy:

Primary

  • KG → Grade 12
  • Grade level required

University

  • Year / level
  • Major / faculty

Higher education

  • BSc / Master / PhD
  • Program / specialization

Shared dimensions for teacher profiles and search filters: market → vertical → grade/level → subject → language of instruction → teacher gender.

06

Teacher onboarding

  1. Public application. Bio, subjects/verticals/grades, qualifications, documents, requested wage/price, intro-video URL.
  2. Moderator review (+ optional interview) → approve / reject / request changes.
  3. Publish & configure. On approval the profile goes live and the teacher sets availability, intro video (YouTube → Vidstack), free-lesson allowance, and custom prices within platform rules.
07

Discovery & search

Students browse and search teachers within their own market. Filters:

  • Subject + grade/level
  • Price and rating
  • Availability / time
  • Teacher gender and language of instruction

A teacher profile shows bio, intro video, subjects/levels, rating & reviews, price, free-lesson offer, and availability.

08

Booking & lesson lifecycle

  1. Request → confirm. Student picks a slot and requests; the teacher must confirm or decline. In-app chat unlocks once the booking exists (to coordinate and share the meeting link).
  2. Delivery. Lesson runs on an external tool — Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or a custom link, attached to the booking.
  3. Reserve. On confirmation the lesson price is reserved from the student's wallet (sufficient balance required to book).
  4. Completion. The student confirms the lesson happened; if no action within a configurable window it auto-completes. Either party can dispute → moderator.
  5. Settlement. On completion the reserved amount is captured, the teacher's wage is credited, and the platform keeps its commission.
  6. Cancellation / no-show. Flexible early, strict late: free up to a configurable cutoff; after it, student no-show forfeits, teacher no-show refunds the student and may incur a penalty. Free trials follow the same rules at zero charge.
09

Pricing & commission

The platform sets a price per lesson category keyed by market + vertical + grade + subject, each with a fixed teacher wage. Example:

CategoryStudent paysTeacher wagePlatform margin
EG · Primary · G4 · Math60 EGP35 EGP25 EGP
  • Teachers may have a per-teacher price override (higher price justified by rating, reviews, volume), approved through admin rules.
  • Commission = student price − teacher wage; revenue also flows via packages/credits and wallet balances.
  • Trials — each teacher configures a number of free lessons (0 to the student, 0 to the teacher; policy-configurable).
10

Payments — manual (launch model)

No gateways at launch. Students pay by bank/wallet transfer to platform accounts and submit a receipt; moderators verify. Three funding flows, all supported:

1 · Wallet top-up

  • Transfer any amount → upload receipt → verify → wallet credited.
  • Bookings draw from wallet.
  • Fewest verifications — default UX.

2 · Pay per booking

  • Transfer exact lesson/package cost at booking.
  • Upload receipt → verify → booking confirmed.

3 · Packages / credits

  • Buy a predefined bundle.
  • Transfer full amount → verify → credits granted.

Flagged priority · Receipt UX

Show the correct platform accounts for the student's market (copy-to-clipboard, amount, reference); upload receipt image/PDF with amount + method; clear status tracking pending → approved / rejected (reason) with notifications; a moderator verification queue with side-by-side receipt view and one-click approve/reject.

Wallet ledger

Track available vs reserved balance with entries for top-up, reservation, capture (settlement), refund, package grant, and adjustment — designed append-only / double-entry-style for auditability.

11

Teacher payouts

  • Scheduled batch (e.g. monthly). The platform accrues each teacher's earned wage from settled lessons.
  • Admin runs a payout cycle: computes per-teacher amounts, transfers manually in local currency, marks the period paid.
  • Teacher sees an earnings dashboard + statements (settled, pending, paid).
12

Reviews & ratings

After a completed lesson, students leave a public rating (1–5) + text review on the teacher. The aggregate shows on the profile and feeds search sort/filter and price-override justification.

13

Messaging

In-app chat is available before booking — students can message a teacher with questions at any time, and the same thread continues after booking to coordinate the session and share the meeting link. Light content filtering to reduce off-platform leakage is a Later consideration.

14

Notifications

Channels at launch: Push (FCM/APNs via Flutter), WhatsApp (Business API), Email. No SMS.

Key events: OTP; booking requested / confirmed / declined; lesson reminders; completion prompt; dispute updates; receipt approved / rejected; wallet credited; package granted; payout completed; review request.

17

Timeline note

The agreed MVP spans a Flutter app + a Next.js teacher portal + a Next.js admin dashboard + a Django API, with a wallet/ledger, manual-payment verification, bilingual RTL, two markets, and three notification channels. That is a large MVP for first launch.

Path A · Recommended

  • Phase the surfaces.
  • Ship student app + admin (manual payments, booking, wallet) first.
  • Teacher portal starts minimal (availability + confirm + earnings), expands as a fast-follow.

Path B

  • Trim scope within each surface.
  • e.g. wallet-only funding first; add pay-per-booking & packages next.

Recommended next deliverables: a concrete phased backlog and an architecture / data-model document.
