Start here

1 · Welcome — what you're looking at

Masaar is a tutoring marketplace, like Preply or Modarby. Students find a vetted teacher, book a live one-to-one lesson, and meet on a video call (Zoom, Meet, Teams). It runs two separate markets — Egypt (EGP) and Saudi Arabia (SAR) — and works fully in both Arabic and English.

This web app is for the two people who run the business behind the scenes:

👩‍🏫 The teacher

Sets up their profile, availability and prices, accepts lesson requests, chats with students, and tracks their earnings.

🛡️ The moderator / admin

Approves new teachers, verifies payments, sets pricing, moderates reviews, and pays teachers out.

Where's the student side?

The student app (searching, booking, paying) is a phone app being built next. You won't test it here — but we've pre-loaded a sample student's activity (a booking request, a chat, a payment receipt) so you can see the teacher and admin sides respond to it.

How this works

2 · Getting started

  1. Open the app link your team shared with you (something like https://masaar-app.vercel.app). It opens in any browser on a computer or phone — nothing to install.
  2. Pick your language at the top of the page — English or العربية. The whole app flips to right-to-left in Arabic.
  3. Sign in with one of the ready-made accounts in the next section.
A note on the addresses in this guide

Throughout, you'll see short paths like /teacher/lessons. These are added to the end of your app link — e.g. https://masaar-app.vercel.app/en/teacher/lessons. But you rarely need to type them: everything is reachable by clicking around from the home screen after you sign in.

Good to know

4 · About sign-up & verification codes

In the finished product, creating an account or resetting a password sends a 6-digit code over WhatsApp. That WhatsApp connection isn't switched on in this test version yet, so those specific screens can't be completed by a tester.

So, for testing:

Use the ready-made accounts above — they're already verified, so you sign straight in with no code needed. You can still look at the Sign up, Verify, and Forgot password screens to review their design; just don't expect to finish them. Everything else in this guide works fully.

The dashboard (home)

Ready
/teacher

Sara's at-a-glance business summary.

Look for

Profile, photo, subjects, availability & prices

Ready
/teacher/profile

Everything a teacher sets up about themselves, on one page.

Try each part
  1. Photo: upload a picture, see it appear, then replace or remove it.
  2. Details: edit the bio (Arabic & English), languages, free-trial count, and paste a YouTube intro link — a video preview appears. Click Save.
  3. Subjects: add a subject she teaches from the list; each shows its price.
  4. Availability: add a weekly time window (a day + start/end time).
  5. Custom price: request a different price for a subject — it shows as pending until an admin approves it (you'll do that in Section 6).
  6. Publish: toggle the profile live. Tip: try publishing with a piece missing to see the helpful "what's needed" message.

Don't skip

7 · Try the whole thing in Arabic

Masaar is built for Arabic-first users. Switching language flips every screen to a proper right-to-left layout — not just translated words, but mirrored menus, forms, tables, and the chat.

Do this
  1. Use the language switch at the top (العربية), or change /en/ to /ar/ in the address.
  2. Revisit two or three screens you already know — the dashboard, the chat, a table — and check the layout reads naturally right-to-left.

Flag anything that looks misaligned, mixes directions awkwardly, or reads oddly in Arabic — this is one of the most valuable things to review.

Your turn

10 · Sending feedback

When something feels off or breaks, a few details make it easy to fix:

  • Which screen — the page name or address (e.g. /teacher/profile), and which account you used.
  • What you did — the steps that led there.
  • What happened vs. what you expected.
  • A screenshot if it's visual, and whether you were in Arabic or English.
Most valuable things to pressure-test

The payment-receipt verification, the full booking flow (request → confirm), the chat, the payout end-to-end, and the Arabic right-to-left layout on every screen.
